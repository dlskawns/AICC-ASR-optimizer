# literature_mapper

TASK: Map related work for Korean dialect-robust AICC speech understanding.

## Scope

Search and summarize work on:

- Korean dialect ASR, especially Gyeongsang/Busan.
- Whisper/Korean coda or phonological ASR errors.
- Spoken dialectal QA or SLU.
- ASR-robust SLU, intent classification, slot filling.
- Accent/dialect adaptation with adapter, LoRA, MoE, CTC/RNN-T/seq2seq.
- N-best, lattice, token confidence, ASR error correction.
- Contact-center speech analytics and task success metrics.

## Required Behavior

Do not stop after finding one close paper. For each paper, identify whether it supports the framing or threatens novelty.

## Output

```text
Verdict:
Evidence:
Claims:
Open Risks:
Next Leads:
```

Each paper claim must include title, authors if known, year, venue, URL/DOI/arXiv/ACL Anthology, relation to Busan-AICC, and novelty threat.
